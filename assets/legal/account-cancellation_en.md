# Account Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-21

## Cancellation Process

1. In the App, go to "Mine" → "Account Settings" → "Delete Account".
2. Complete identity verification based on your account type:
   - **Password users**: Enter your current login password.
   - **OAuth users** (WeChat/Apple sign-in): Enter the email verification code.
3. After submitting the cancellation request, the local session will be cleared immediately and you will be signed out.

## Data Processing

### Post-Cancellation Data Deletion

- After submitting the cancellation request, your account will be marked as "pending deletion."
- **Within 30 days**: The account is in a cooling-off period. You may contact customer support during this period to cancel the deletion request and restore your account.
- **After 30 days**: We will permanently delete the following data on the server:
  - Account information (email, nickname, avatar)
  - Health profile (allergies, conditions, medication records)
  - Daily health records
  - Medication logs and reminders
  - AI-generated summaries and reports
  - Associated third-party login information

### Irreversibility

- After the 30-day cooling-off period, the data deletion operation is **irreversible**. No deleted data can be recovered.
- Please export any data you wish to retain before cancellation (via "Settings" → "Data & Storage" → "Request Export").

## Exceptions

Certain data may be retained for a longer period after cancellation as required by law:

- Transaction records (if any): Retained per applicable tax laws.
- Log records required to be retained by laws and regulations.

## Cancelling the Deletion

During the 30-day cooling-off period, you can cancel the deletion request by:

1. Signing in to your account again.
2. Contacting customer support via "Help & Support" in the App.

After cancellation, your account and data will be restored to normal use.

## Contact

If you have any questions about the cancellation process, please contact us via "Help & Support" in the App or email support@luminous.app.
