# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-11

## 1. Introduction

Luminous (the "App") takes user privacy seriously. This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information.

## 2. Information Collection

We collect the following types of personal information:

### 2.1 Registration Information
- Email address
- Nickname
- Encrypted password (only hash value is stored)

### 2.2 Health Data
- Daily health records (symptoms, mood, sleep, etc.)
- Medication records and reminders
- Allergy history and health conditions
- Health report data

### 2.3 Device Information
- Device model and operating system version
- Application usage logs (for troubleshooting)

## 3. Information Use

We use your personal information to:

- Provide personalized health management services
- Send medication reminders and notifications
- Generate health reports and trend analysis
- Improve app functionality and user experience
- Provide customer support

## 4. Information Storage and Security

- Your data is stored on secure cloud servers with TLS encryption during transmission.
- Passwords are hashed using the bcrypt algorithm.
- Access is strictly controlled; only authorized personnel can access user data.
- We conduct regular security audits and vulnerability patching.

## 5. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Exceptions include:

- Your explicit consent
- Legal requirements
- Protecting the legitimate rights of the App, users, or the public

## 6. User Rights

You have the right to:

- Access your personal information
- Correct inaccurate information
- Delete your account and related data
- Export your data
- Withdraw consent

## 7. Third-Party Services

The App may use the following third-party services:

- WeChat Open Platform (for OAuth login)
- Push notification services
- Data analytics services

Each third-party service has its own privacy policy. We recommend you read the relevant terms.

## 8. Minor Protection

The App is not intended for minors under 14. If we discover registration information of minors without guardian consent, we will delete it.

## 9. Policy Updates

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Updated policies will be published in the App, and major changes will be communicated via notifications.

## 10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about the privacy policy, please contact us through "Help & Support" in the App.
