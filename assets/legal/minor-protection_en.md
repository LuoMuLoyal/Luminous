# Minor Protection Statement

Last updated: 2026-07-21

## General Provisions

Luminous (hereinafter "this App") attaches great importance to the protection of minors' personal information. In accordance with relevant laws and regulations, this statement aims to inform minors and their guardians about the rules for processing minors' information.

## Applicable Users

This App is a health management application primarily designed for adult users. If you are a minor under the age of 18, please use this App under the guidance and consent of your guardian, and carefully read this statement and the Privacy Policy.

## Guardian Consent Mechanism

1. Before children under the age of 14 use this App, their guardian should read and agree to this statement and the Privacy Policy.
2. The App may only be registered and used after guardian consent is obtained.
3. Guardians have the right to withdraw consent at any time, after which we will delete the minor's personal information.

## Information Processing Rules for Minors

### Scope of Information Collection

For minors, we only collect the following necessary information:

- Basic health records (e.g., medication reminders, health journals)
- Essential account information (e.g., nickname)

We **do not** collect the following information from minors:

- Precise location data
- Contacts/address book
- Biometric information such as facial recognition

### Information Usage Restrictions

- Minors' information is used solely for providing basic health management features.
- Minors' information will not be used for personalized advertising.
- Minors' information will not be shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

## Content Protection

- This App does not provide content that may harm the physical or mental health of minors.
- The AI assistant applies additional safety filters for conversations related to minors.

## Guardian Rights

Guardians have the right to:

1. **Access**: View the personal information collected from the minor.
2. **Correct**: Request correction of inaccurate information.
3. **Delete**: Request deletion of the minor's personal information.
4. **Withdraw Consent**: Withdraw previously given consent at any time.

To exercise these rights, please contact us via "Help & Support" in the App.

## Contact

If you have any questions about this statement, please contact us via "Help & Support" in the App or email support@luminous.app.
