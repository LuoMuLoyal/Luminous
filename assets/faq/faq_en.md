# Luminous FAQ

## Does my data sync to the cloud?

Yes, after signing in your health profile, medication records, and reminder settings automatically sync to the cloud so you can use them across devices. When not signed in, data is stored locally on your device only.

## How do I change medication reminder times?

Go to "Settings → Notifications → Reminder settings" to adjust medication reminder times. You can also set individual reminders for each medicine.

## Are AI suggestions accurate?

AI suggestions are generated based on your health profile and medication data for reference only. They cannot replace professional medical advice. For medication safety decisions, please consult a doctor or pharmacist.

## What if I forget my password?

On the login page, tap "Forgot password" and use your registered email to receive a verification code to reset your password. If you also cannot access your email, please contact us via Feedback.

## How do I export my visit report?

Go to "Settings → Data Export" to request a visit report PDF. Once processing is complete, a download link will be generated and you can save the PDF locally.

## Is my health data secure?

Your health data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and is visible only to you. You will be prompted to confirm before sharing any data, and you can revoke authorization at any time. See the Privacy Policy for details.
